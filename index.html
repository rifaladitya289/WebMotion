SUPPORT

Help & Feedback

Understand preview limitations and share feedback.

Why might a website not load?

A website can block being displayed inside another page using CSP frame-ancestors or X-Frame-Options. Browser extensions, authentication, network errors, and mixed-content rules can also affect previews. WebMotion does not bypass these restrictions.

What can WebMotion inspect?

The browser's same-origin policy prevents this app from reading most cross-origin website content. Responsive viewport controls work on the frame, but detailed animation and DOM inspection may be unavailable for external sites. Use the manual checklists when needed.
